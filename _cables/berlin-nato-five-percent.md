GERMAN FOREIGN OFFICE — ROUTINE

FROM: BERLIN  
TO: WASHINGTON STATION  
REF: NATO HAGUE SUMMIT, JUNE 24–25, 2025  

Germany has agreed to spend 5 percent of GDP on defence by 2035. This cable documents how we reached this position and what we expect it to mean.

In 2006, NATO members agreed to spend 2 percent of GDP on defence. Germany did not meet this target for seventeen years. In 2024, Germany met it. In 2025, we agreed to a new target that is two and a half times higher.

The President of the United States has described this outcome as proof that his negotiating style produces results. This characterisation is not without foundation.

The 5 percent target includes 1.5 percent allocated to "defence-related spending," a category whose definition has not been finalised. This flexibility was considered essential by several delegations, including our own.

Germany's current defence budget is approximately 2.1 percent of GDP. The additional 2.9 percent required by 2035 represents a sum that the Finance Ministry has not yet identified a source for. The Finance Ministry has been informed of the commitment. Their response is pending.

The summit was described by the US President as "low-drama." This assessment is shared.

— BERLIN STATION
