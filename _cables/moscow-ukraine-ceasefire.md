MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION

FROM: MOSCOW  
TO: WASHINGTON STATION  
REF: US ENVOY CONSULTATIONS, FEBRUARY–MARCH 2025  

The US Special Envoy has now visited Moscow twice. This cable records what was proposed, what was not proposed, and the significance of the difference.

The Envoy proposed a ceasefire along current lines of control. The Envoy did not propose NATO membership for Ukraine. The Envoy proposed that Ukraine receive security guarantees from European nations rather than the United States. This represents a meaningful reconfiguration of the postwar European security architecture. The Envoy did not appear to present this as a concession.

The US President has stated that the war will be over within twenty-four hours of taking office. This did not occur. The President has not updated this timeline publicly. The war continues. This is not a complaint. This is an observation.

Our position on territorial gains is unchanged. Our position on NATO non-membership for Ukraine is unchanged. Our assessment is that the current US administration's positions are compatible with our minimum requirements. Whether they are compatible with Ukrainian minimum requirements is a separate question, and not one we are positioned to resolve.

The Envoy was cordial. The meetings were substantive. We are waiting.

— MOSCOW STATION
