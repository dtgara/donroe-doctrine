CANADA FOREIGN AFFAIRS — PRIORITY

FROM: OTTAWA  
TO: WASHINGTON STATION  
REF: TRUTH SOCIAL POSTS, JAN 7–13, 2025  

The subject of the United States President's repeated statements regarding Canadian statehood has now been raised in three separate cabinet sessions. This cable attempts to establish a working assessment.

The statements are as follows: Canada should become the 51st state. Prime Minister Trudeau should be referred to as Governor Trudeau. Economic force — not military force — will be used. This position has not been withdrawn.

Our preliminary assessment is that the statements are not intended as literal policy. Our revised assessment, conducted after the fourth repetition in five days, is that we are not certain.

The Prime Minister has responded with the phrase "not going to happen." This has not prompted a retraction. The phrase "economic force" remains undefined. Our trade dependency on the United States is approximately 75 percent of exports.

RECOMMENDATION: Do not escalate. Do not accommodate. Identify the precise threshold between these two positions and remain there for an indeterminate period. Report any movement on the US side immediately.

The moose on the G7 commemorative coin has been noted. No formal response is required at this time.

— OTTAWA STATION
