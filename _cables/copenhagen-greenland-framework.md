DANISH MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

FROM: COPENHAGEN  
TO: WASHINGTON STATION  
REF: DAVOS STATEMENTS, JANUARY 21–22, 2026  

The US President stated at Davos that a "framework of a future deal" on Greenland has been reached. Denmark stated that Greenland is not for sale. Both statements were made within the same 48-hour period. This cable explains how this is possible.

The framework, as described by the President, involves "total access" with "no end" and "no time limit," secured "at no cost." Denmark has not agreed to these terms. The framework the President described and the framework Denmark has agreed to are, at present, different documents, or possibly the same document interpreted in incompatible ways.

NATO Secretary General Rutte's role in the Davos meeting has added complexity. Mr Rutte indicated that the framework would involve increased allied Arctic security commitments. He confirmed that Greenland's mineral resources were not discussed. We note that the President's subsequent description of the framework did not include these qualifications.

Greenland's government has stated that the island is not for sale. The Greenland government's statement has not prompted a retraction of the President's statement.

Our recommended posture remains: reiterate sovereignty, avoid confrontation, and note with interest that the tariff threats that accompanied earlier statements have been withdrawn. This is progress of a specific and limited kind.

The word "absurd" was used in our initial response in January 2025. We are reserving it for future use.

— COPENHAGEN STATION
