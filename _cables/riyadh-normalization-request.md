KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA — MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS — EYES ONLY

FROM: RIYADH  
TO: WASHINGTON STATION  
REF: OVAL OFFICE MEETING, CROWN PRINCE VISIT, NOVEMBER 2025  

The Crown Prince's meeting with the US President last week can be characterised as follows: productive in tone, inconclusive in substance, and notably direct regarding Israeli-Saudi normalisation.

The President indicated that he expects all Abraham Accords non-signatories to normalise relations with Israel following the resolution of the Iran situation. The Crown Prince indicated that this expectation is understood. The Crown Prince further indicated that it will not be met under current conditions. The meeting reportedly became tense at this point.

The Kingdom's position remains: normalisation requires a credible, irreversible pathway to Palestinian statehood. The current Israeli government's position on this matter is a matter of public record.

The President has stated that Saudi Arabia "wants to make a deal very badly." The Crown Prince did not contest this characterisation in the meeting. The Kingdom does want a deal. The Kingdom wants a different deal.

The Gaza situation, the Lebanon campaign, and the Iran strikes have altered regional public opinion in ways that constrain the Crown Prince's domestic flexibility. This is not an argument. This is an explanation of facts.

We will continue to engage. We will not normalise on the current timeline. These positions are compatible.

— RIYADH STATION
