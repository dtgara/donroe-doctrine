Register status: ongoing. Covers documented foreign business interests identified through financial disclosures, press releases, investigative reporting, and congressional investigations. Undisclosed deals and verbal agreements not yet in the public record are not included.

Distinct from The Ledger: The Ledger documents payments received by the Trump family from foreign entities. This register documents assets, stakes, and developments the Trump family is building in countries subject to US foreign policy — a structural conflict of interest that exists independently of any single transaction.
