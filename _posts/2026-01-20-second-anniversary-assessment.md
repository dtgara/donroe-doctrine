One year into the second term, the archive's data tables contain the following: 31 tracked predictions (7 resolved correct, 4 resolved wrong, 1 abandoned, 19 pending), 39 documented deals (of which 14 are classified ACTIVE, 18 UNRESOLVED, 5 COMPLETED, and 2 SUPERSEDED), 18 international agreement withdrawals, and 53 documented contradictions between statements made by the same administration.

These numbers are not judgements. They are a ledger.

**On predictions**

The most frequently cited prediction from the first year of the second term — that the Russia-Ukraine war would end within 24 hours of inauguration — has been reclassified as abandoned. The archive defines abandoned as a prediction whose time horizon has passed and which the source has ceased to reference. The war continues. The Special Envoy has visited Moscow twice. A ceasefire proposal remains on the table.

The Iran prediction — that Iran would make a deal — is currently in a different category. Negotiations have been ongoing since spring 2025. A memorandum of understanding appears closer than at any point in the preceding year. The archive does not update status until outcomes are confirmed.

**On withdrawals**

The United States withdrew from or froze participation in 66 international organisations via executive memorandum on January 7, 2026 — the second anniversary of the January 6 Capitol events. Several of these withdrawals duplicate first-term withdrawals that were reversed by the Biden administration. The archive tracks these as second withdrawal events. The withdrawals register now covers organisations across environment, health, security, trade, and multilateral governance categories.

**On the doctrine itself**

The doctrine's defining characteristic, one year in, is not any single position but the accumulation of positions. Countries that were threatened have made deals. Countries that made deals have been threatened again. International institutions that were defunded have not closed. Allies that were pressured have increased defence spending. Adversaries that were courted have not become allies.

The archive does not assess whether this constitutes success. It records that the cycle continues, and that it is the same cycle.
