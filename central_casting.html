Register status: ongoing. Entries cover documented remarks identified through transcripts, White House pool reports, and verified press coverage. Commentary on domestic US officials (press secretaries, cabinet nominees) is deliberately excluded even where the same rhetorical habit applies, to keep this register scoped to foreign heads of state, heads of government, and senior foreign officials.

Role classification: head_of_state · head_of_government · military · royal_non_head · diplomatic · other_senior — captures officials who are senior but not the nominal head of their country, a distinction this register treats as significant rather than incidental.

Valence: complimentary · ambiguous · derogatory — left blank where a remark is extensive praise but never touches on appearance (see Asim Munir), logged as a deliberate contrast rather than force-fit onto the compliment/insult axis.
